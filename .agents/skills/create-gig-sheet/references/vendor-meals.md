# Vendor Meal Coordination

Use this private coordination check for every wedding, including revisions after
a timeline change. The goal is to prove that every Greenway performer and crew
member who needs a meal can eat without missing a required duty.

## Non-negotiable sequence

For the band, the meal must occur after band soundcheck finishes and before the
first dinner set. It must not overlap any required performance, special, readiness,
or transition time. A safe window may fall after earlier reception specials and
before a later full set. When no dinner set is confirmed, identify and confirm the
applicable next band set or call instead of inventing one.

For ceremony, cocktail-hour, guest, and other supporting musicians, arrange a
separate feasible window outside each person's own setup, soundcheck, and
performance duties. It may occur after the person's last duty only when that
person will remain on site and food will still be ready to eat. Do not force all
roles into the band's window or assume anyone will stay through guest dinner.

Guest dinner is not evidence of a vendor meal. Confirm all of these explicitly:

- Meal-service start and end.
- Room/access and whether ready-to-eat food remains available for the full window.
- Each role's soundcheck or final pre-meal duty end.
- The band's first dinner set or applicable next confirmed set/call, plus each
  supporting role's next required duty when one follows.
- The planner- or caterer-confirmed ready-by or return time when another duty follows.
- The final meal count by role.

Do not invent a meal duration or infer a safe window from gaps in the guest
timeline. When the meal window, soundcheck end, next call, room, availability,
ready-by time, or count is missing, keep the meal `TBD` and add a private action
to obtain it.

## Counts

The core band baseline is band musicians + 1 sound engineer. Track any ceremony,
cocktail-hour, guest, or specialty performers separately. Ask whether each person
needs a meal, add only confirmed needs, and deduplicate anyone serving in more
than one role. Confirm the final roster and any dietary or access restrictions.
Keep counts private and never display them on the gig sheet.

## Status

- `PASS`: Start and end, room/access/availability, soundcheck or setup end, the
  band's first dinner set or applicable next confirmed set/call, supporting-role
  next duties and ready-by times when applicable, and deduplicated count are explicit.
  The full interval is clear of every duty for each included role.
- `UNCONFIRMED`: One or more required facts are missing. The pages may remain a
  local draft with `Meals: TBD`, but do not describe meal timing or final gig
  readiness as confirmed.
- `CONFLICT`: A known meal interval overlaps a required duty or cannot meet the
  agreed ready-by time. This blocks release until the planner supplies a safe
  window or changes the duty schedule.

Re-run the check whenever any soundcheck, ceremony, cocktail hour, special,
set, or meal time changes. A known overlap is a release blocker even when the
meal time came from a final planner timeline.

## Private checkpoints

- As personnel are confirmed: collect each person's need and restrictions. Use
  any earlier planner or caterer headcount deadline when it falls before D-7.
- Seven days before the wedding: confirm needs by person and request the exact
  window, room, availability, ready-by time, and accommodated count. D-7 is
  Greenway's internal default confirmation deadline, not a client promise or
  permission to miss an earlier catering cutoff.
- Two days before the wedding: recheck timing, roster, deduplicated count, and
  conflicts. D-2 is not automatic permission to add or buy meals.
- After any timeline change: run the sequence check again immediately.

Record the checkpoints and outreach status in the private wedding notes. Do not
send messages, create cloud drafts, schedule external reminders, spend money, or
accept meal charges without Adrian's approval. If Adrian says he will ask, prepare
concise ready-to-use text and mark outreach pending.

## Verification before issue

For every Greenway role, write one private line in this form:

`Role | duty ends | meal start-end | room/access/availability | ready by | next duty | count | status`

Verify the band's window against the end of soundcheck, the first dinner set or
applicable next confirmed set/call, and every required special, readiness, and
transition inside that span.
For every supporting role, verify a separate window outside that person's duties,
including an after-duty window only when the person will stay and food remains
available. Then compare the final private count to the planner's accommodated
count. Show only the verified time and room on performer pages; otherwise show
`TBD`.
