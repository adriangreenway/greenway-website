Operational Notes
No ceremony music. The Co-Cathedral organist covers the church; the band is not at the ceremony.
Cocktail hour has no live musicians. Greenway provides a speaker in the cocktail-hour lobby and Adrian builds the playlist: simple, light background music — jazz and acoustic-pop love songs/covers.
All recorded specials run through the Greenway PA. There is no outside DJ or emcee.
Edelweiss must be the exact recording: Bill Lee & Charmian Carr, The Sound of Music 50th Anniversary — the groom's mother's specific request.
Couple's must-play: “You Never Call Me By My Name” — David Allan Coe. Their requests not in our catalog go on the break playlist (their instruction): Mirrors, Dancing Queen, Rattlin' Bog, Gimme Gimme Gimme (sax feature), Mr. Brightside.
First dance, father/daughter, and mother/son are each only 45 sec to 1 min — use the cut times listed above.
No bouquet or garter toss. Cake cutting 7:25 PM, top tier not saved.
Artists they love — Andrew: Foo Fighters, Green Day, Ian Munsick, Shane Smith & the Saints, Billy Joel, Bruce Springsteen, Cody Johnson. Katherine: Justin Bieber, Brett Eldredge, Chris Young, Cody Johnson, Zac Brown Band, trending pop & R&B.
Break OverlapThe 7:20 PM parent dances sit inside the listed 7:00–7:25 band break. Father/daughter is live — band must be back on stage before 7:20 PM.
