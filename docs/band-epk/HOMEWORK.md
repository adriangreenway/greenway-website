## Video homework (the reel is only as good as this)

2. **10 to 20 clips, 30 seconds or longer each**, so there are windows to cut from. More is better.
3. Cover these moments across clips:
   - Packed dance floor with the crowd visible in frame
   - Full band wide shot on stage (everyone in frame at once)
   - A high energy song peak (horn hits, big chorus)
   - A slow moment: first dance or dinner set
   - Ceremony or cocktail hour if the band does those
